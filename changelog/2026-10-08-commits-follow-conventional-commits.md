## 2026-10-08 — Commits follow Conventional Commits

Commit subjects here were plain sentences. microfrontend-learn and ai-news-reader use Conventional Commits, and
the same style now applies to all three repos. The types also match the branch prefixes the global rules already
require (`feat/`, `fix/`, …).

Rules and skills count as the product, so they get `feat`, `fix` or `refactor`, never `docs`. Otherwise almost every
commit would be `docs`, and the type would say nothing. Older commits keep their plain subjects.
